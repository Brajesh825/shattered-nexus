# ⏳ Character Design: Chloe (The Weaver of Hours)

## 🏛️ Identity & Narrative (Curator)
- **Name**: Chloe
- **ID**: `chloe`
- **Origin**: The Timeless Archive (Summoned Stranger)
- **Title**: The Weaver of Hours
- **Inspiration**: Chloe Aubert (Tensei Slime)
- **Lore**: A traveler who walked the "Gaps between Seconds." She arrived in the Nexus from a world that has already ceased to exist. She carries the **Crystalline Chronometer**, a blade that contains the literal sand of her home world's last hour.

## 🛡️ Mechanical Blueprint (Aegis)
### Base Stats
| Stat | Value | Note |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **HP** | 68 | Durable for an agile unit |
| **MP** | 42 | High skill ceiling |
| **ATK** | 19 | Strong physical base |
| **DEF** | 13 | Standard light armor |
| **SPD** | 24 | **Elite Tier** - Highest in current roster |
| **MAG** | 18 | Secondary scaling for Time-skills |
| **LCK** | 12 | Average |

### Passive: Chronos Resonance
- **Innate**: +15% Physical Evasion.
- **Effect**: Every successful dodge grants a **+5% SPD** buff (Stacks up to 3 times, duration: Battle).

### Initial Skillset (Draft)
1. **Temporal Strike**: Physical hit. 10% chance to inflict **Slow** for 2 turns.
2. **Hourglass Barrier**: Buff. Grants an ally 25% Evasion for 1 turn.
3. **Shattered Second**: (Ultimate) Chloe disappears and strikes all enemies 3 times with light-sand shards.

## 🎨 Visual Specification (Vivid)
- **Art Style**: Vivid Standard (High-Fidelity Illustrious).
- **Primary Weapon**: **The Crystalline Chronometer**. A rapier-style blade made of semi-translucent glass. Inside the blade, glowing golden sand flows constantly toward the tip.
- **VFX Theme**: Golden sand particles, purple chronal gears, and teal motion trails from her cape.
- **Sprite Frames (2x3 Grid)**:
    - `[0,0] Idle`: Calm, cape swaying, sword sheathed in light.
    - `[1,0] Ready`: Crouched low, sword held in reverse grip.
    - `[2,0] Attack`: A forward thrust that leaves a trail of golden sand.
    - `[0,1] Skill`: Holding the blade vertically as it glows with intense purple light.
    - `[1,1] Hurt`: Sword used as a shield, glass-like shards flying off.
    - `[2,1] Fallen`: Kneeling, the blade shattered into sand on the ground.
